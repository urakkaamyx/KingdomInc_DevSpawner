[b]DevSpawner - 1.3.0[/b]
Generate any item without normal production inputs using Kingdom Inc's built-in [b]Spawner[/b]. This is a [b]cheat[/b].

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Unlock DevSpawner for free in Tech Tree > Mods, then build Spawner from the Resource menu.
[*]Click the + or selected item icon to open a searchable item picker.
[*]Choose any item, including locked items, with names, icons and gold values shown in the picker.
[*]Gold values are for 100% quality before shop bonuses. Unsellable items say Not sellable.
[*]The selected item's icon and name appear on the node. Hover for its gold value.
[*]Your selection is saved. Existing kingdoms with placed Spawners keep access automatically.
[/list]

[b]Requirements[/b]
Foundation 0.5.0 or newer and [url=https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx/releases/tag/v5.4.23.6]BepInEx 5[/url], installed separately.

[b]Installation[/b]
1. Close Kingdom Inc and install the requirements if needed.
2. Extract this mod's ZIP beside KingdomInc.exe.
3. The plugin belongs at BepInEx/plugins/DevSpawner/DevSpawner.dll.
4. Start the game. Open Tech Tree > Mods and unlock DevSpawner for free.
5. Build Spawner from the Resource menu. Normal building costs apply.

[b]Choosing an item[/b]
Click the Spawner's [b]+[/b] to open the picker. Search by item name or ID and click a row to choose what it makes. Click the selected item's icon to choose again.

Changing items clears pending output and keeps connected pipes. Selecting the same item keeps pending output. Close the picker, press Escape, right-click or click outside to cancel. An unset Spawner can also learn its item from a connected input.

[b]Updating and removal[/b]
Close the game before replacing or deleting the DLL. Remove BepInEx/plugins/DevSpawner/DevSpawner.dll to disable it. Existing Spawners keep their selected item but stop generating. Items already produced remain in your kingdom.

[b]Version 1.3.0[/b]
Adds the free DevSpawner unlock to Tech Tree > Mods through Foundation.
